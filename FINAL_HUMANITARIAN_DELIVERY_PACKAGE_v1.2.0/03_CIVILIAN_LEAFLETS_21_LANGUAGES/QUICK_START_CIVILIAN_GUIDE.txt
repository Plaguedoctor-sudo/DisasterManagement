DISASTER MANAGEMENT APP - OFFLINE EMERGENCY GUIDE & INSTALLATION
================================================================================
Platform: Android 10+ (API 29+)
Zero Cloud / Zero Internet / Zero Cellular Service Required

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO INSTALL (NO INTERNET NEEDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Locate the file: DisasterManagement-v1.2.0-signed.apk
2. Tap on the file to begin installation.
3. If Android prompts: "Install unknown apps from this source":
   - Tap "Settings"
   - Switch "Allow from this source" to ON
   - Tap "Back" and tap "Install"
4. The application icon (tactical shield) will appear on your home screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO SHARE TO OTHER PHONES (VIRAL OFFLINE SHARING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Method A:
1. Open DisasterManagement on your phone.
2. Tap "Share App Offline" in the menu.
3. It creates an offline Wi-Fi hotspot and displays a QR code.
4. Have your friend connect their phone's Wi-Fi to your hotspot and scan the QR
   code with their camera. The APK will download directly between your phones!

Method B:
1. Open your File Manager -> Downloads.
2. Select DisasterManagement-v1.2.0-signed.apk.
3. Tap "Share" and send via Bluetooth or Nearby Share / Quick Share.
